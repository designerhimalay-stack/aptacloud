How we work

Howourapplicationre-engineeringserviceswork.

Re-architecting critical business applications is complex. Our process gives it structure, predictability, and expertise.

01

Audit and analysis

Our engineers examine your current application environment in depth: code, architecture, data flows, and integrations.

02

Design and planning

Detailed specifications for the future architecture, components, data structures, APIs, and user experience.

03

Engineering and migration

Agile teams build, integrate, and validate the re-engineered application against the blueprint, then migrate and test the data.

04

Post-implementation support

Continued support after deployment to tune performance and meet new needs as they arise.

Use cases

Usecases.

Where re-engineering pays off in the industries we work in.

Oil & Gas

Operations teams often run decades-old plant and field systems that disagree with the ERP. Re-engineering them onto one cloud data platform keeps the data in step and lays the foundation for AI forecasting.

Engineering & Construction

Project teams often depend on desktop tools that office, site, and client cannot share. Re-engineering them into secure cloud applications gives everyone the same current information.

Insurance

Insurers often serve customers and brokers through web and mobile channels built on outdated frameworks. Re-platforming them adds responsive design and self-service while the core systems stay in place.
