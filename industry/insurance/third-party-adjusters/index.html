Typical engagement

Multi-carrier TPAs

TPAs often receive claims notices through many channels in different formats, with manual validation holding adjusters up and every carrier's system working differently. We automate intake, validation, and routing for one carrier first, then connect the others through APIs, with one set of dashboards across all of them.

What changes

  • Faster claims processing
  • More adjuster time on claims, less on admin
  • Central reporting across carriers
