Challenges

Thedigitalchallengeslimitinginsuranceperformance.

Insurers often run technology built up over many years. Legacy platforms, disconnected applications, scattered data, and manual processes make modernisation hard and slow innovation down.

Legacy systems that are hard to modernise

Long-running applications and mainframes hold critical business logic, which makes new technology hard to adopt.

Disconnected insurance applications

Policy, claims, billing, CRM, document, and enterprise systems run separately, creating silos and slow workflows.

Scattered insurance data

Data spread across legacy applications, databases, spreadsheets, and departments makes one view of customers, policies, and claims hard to reach.

Manual claims and operations

Manual hand-offs and repetitive workflows add processing time, create errors, and limit productivity.

Typical engagements

Whataninsuranceengagementlookslike.

How first projects usually run for carriers, MGAs, and TPAs.

Typical engagement

Carriers: policy administration modernisation

We often see carriers whose policy administration system barely talks to claims or billing, so a new product means weeks of manual coordination. We prove the approach on one product line, then modernise the core in phases and connect it to claims, billing, and CRM on Microsoft Azure.

What changes

  • One view of the policy lifecycle
  • Products configured and launched faster
  • Less reconciliation between systems
Typical engagement

MGAs: AI submission triage

Underwriters at MGAs often triage every submission by hand against appetite guidelines. We start with a proof of concept on one intake channel, scoring and routing submissions with Azure OpenAI, then roll it out across brokers and lines.

What changes

  • Faster quote turnaround for brokers
  • More consistent appetite decisions
  • Underwriters focused on the risks worth writing
Typical engagement

TPAs: connected claims intake

TPAs often receive claims from several carriers in different formats, validated by hand. We automate intake and validation for one carrier's claims first, then connect the other carriers through APIs.

What changes

  • Faster claims intake and validation
  • One view of claims across carriers
  • Adjusters freed from manual checks
