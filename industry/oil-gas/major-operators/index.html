Challenges

The engineering barriers limiting operational scale.

SCADA and EAM operate in isolation

Maintenance teams work without live telemetry because SCADA and enterprise asset management exchange data inconsistently.

Operational data is trapped across systems

Production platforms, historians, GIS, IoT, inspections, and drilling systems produce data that never comes together.

Reactive maintenance reduces availability

Without remote telemetry, engineers cannot see equipment degrading until it fails.

Compliance depends on manual reconciliation

Engineers spend their time validating records between systems instead of reporting from governed data.
