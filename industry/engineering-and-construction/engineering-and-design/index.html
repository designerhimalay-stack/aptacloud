Challenges

The challenges limiting design efficiency and collaboration.

Disconnected design systems create data silos

CAD, BIM, PLM, and document platforms work apart, so design data drifts out of step.

Manual workflows slow project delivery

Reviews, approvals, revisions, and document exchanges are handled by hand.

Limited visibility across engineering data

Drawings, models, specifications, and records live in separate systems, with no single source of truth.

Design changes increase execution risk

Uncoordinated revisions and slow updates affect schedule, cost, and quality.
