Challenges

Theoperationalchallengesdelayingconstructionprojects.

Disconnected project systems reduce visibility

Project management, scheduling, ERP, procurement, BIM, and field apps sit in silos, so performance cannot be analysed in real time.

Manual field reporting slows decisions

Paper inspections, scattered punch lists, and slow site updates delay the response to problems on site.

Limited resource and equipment visibility

Without connected tracking for crews and materials, project managers cannot allocate resources well.

Fragmented safety and compliance records

Inspections, quality audits, permits, and compliance records spread across systems add admin and risk.
