What we do

AWScostoptimisationservices.

Expert AWS optimisation through cloud resource and cost management, to cut waste, use resources well, and find savings.

A strategic optimisation framework

A plan that fits your business objectives, resources, and growth, for cost control from the start.

Resource utilisation analysis

Audits of EC2, RDS, Lambda, and other resources to find what sits idle, then right-sizing for better performance and less overspend.

Governance and compliance

Identity and access policies, security controls, and AWS Config rules, so your environment stays safe and compliant.

Application performance

Storage, compute, databases, and subscriptions right-sized with S3 tiers, EBS volumes, and container workload placement, without raising costs.

Monitoring and protection

Multi-factor authentication, identity-based access, Amazon CloudWatch, and Amazon GuardDuty, so unauthorised activity never drives your bill up.

Ongoing optimisation

A regular practice, not a one-off: we review usage, find idle instances, and act on Trusted Advisor and Cost Explorer findings.

Ourprocess.

Five stages, ending in regular reviews.

    Assessment

  2. 02
