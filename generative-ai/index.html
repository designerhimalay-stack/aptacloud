Use cases

WheregenerativeAIchangeskeybusinessoperations.

We know how to put generative AI to work in enterprise systems. These are the use cases where we see it pay off.

01

Customer service automation

Want to give customers better support? Here's how we use AI in customer service:

  • AI chat assistants that answer customer questions in real time.
  • Automatic ticket summaries, so your agents resolve queries faster.
  • Routing customers to the right resources for a better experience.

Automating support can cut response times sharply while keeping the personal touch customers expect.

02

Marketing and sales enablement

Speed up your marketing with generative AI. Here's how we use it:

  • Drafting campaign content aimed at your ideal customers.
  • Personalising customer interactions at scale.
  • Optimising the performance of your marketing campaigns.

Generative AI helps you analyse customer behaviour and run campaigns that convert better across every touchpoint.

03

Operations optimisation

Lower operational overheads and make processes more efficient. Here's how:

  • AI document processing that frees up your people's time.
  • Reports generated and delivered to stakeholders automatically.
  • Manual, time-intensive processes turned into automated workflows.

Your business saves time and money, and your teams can focus on strategic work.

04

Knowledge management

Get the full value of your organisation's knowledge with AI:

  • AI search that helps employees find what they need in seconds.
  • AI summaries that give people quick insight from long documents.
  • Insights that surface hidden patterns in your data.

Better access to knowledge improves efficiency and lifts productivity.
